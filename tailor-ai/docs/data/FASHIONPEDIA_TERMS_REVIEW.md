# Fashionpedia Terms and Image-Rights Review

**Reviewed:** 2026-08-06  
**Intended use:** Private, non-commercial capstone research and prototype evaluation.

- Fashionpedia annotations, ontology, and website content are stated as CC BY 4.0. Attribution and citation obligations apply.
- Official repository software is governed by the BSD-style terms shown by the project. No Fashionpedia code was copied or installed here.
- Fashionpedia does not own the underlying image copyrights. The official terms require compliance with the respective Flickr, Unsplash, Burst, Freestocks, Kaboompics, and Pexels terms.

The acquired annotation metadata exposes per-image licence IDs, including CC licences, public-domain, provider licences, and an `all rights reserved/not found` state. This supports image-level filtering but does not create one collective image licence. Images with NoDerivatives identifiers or `all rights reserved/not found` must be excluded from generated crops pending specific review. Original images and crops remain private, outside Git, and must not be redistributed. Future commercial use and trained-weight distribution require a new review.

Citation: Jia et al., “Fashionpedia: Ontology, Segmentation, and an Attribute Localization Dataset,” ECCV 2020.
